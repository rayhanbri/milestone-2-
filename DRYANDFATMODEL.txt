
# Interview Questions

## What is DRY?
Don't repead Yourself

A principle that avoids repeating the same logic/code multiple times.use reusable functions, or utilities ro hooks

---

## What is Fat Model?

Keeping business logic inside model/service layer instead of controller.

---
## Why use DRY?

To improve maintainability and reduce duplication.

---
## Why keep controllers thin?

Because controllers should only handle request and response.
---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Repeating validation logic
❌ Huge controllers
❌ Copy-paste code
❌ Database queries everywhere
❌ Business logic inside routes

---

# Best Practice Structure

```txt
Route
 ↓
Controller
 ↓
Service
 ↓
Model
 ↓
Database
```

This structure is common in scalable backend applications.
